About The Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test

The Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test is a fictional decision-making experience designed to answer one question: How would you actually perform if the world collapsed?

Instead of simply asking whether you could fight zombies, the test puts you through a series of situations involving resources, shelter, danger, other survivors, planning, difficult choices, and long-term survival.

How It Works

The full test contains 90 decisions. Each situation presents four possible choices, and your decisions affect your final results.

Your choices are evaluated primarily through a Survival score. The final version of the test will also include a separate Morality score based only on decisions that reveal something meaningful about your values.

Survival Score

Survival measures how effective your decisions are from a practical survival perspective.

Your decisions can involve preparation, resource management, risk assessment, shelter, mobility, health, security, cooperation, and long-term planning.

The full test contains 90 decisions with a maximum Survival score of 450 points. Your final score is converted into a percentage.

Morality Score

Morality is a separate part of the test.

Not every survival decision has a moral dimension. Therefore, morality points are assigned only to questions where the available choices genuinely reveal something about how you treat other people, responsibility, loyalty, fairness, compassion, or similar values.

This means that choosing the most practical survival option does not automatically make someone more or less moral.

There Isn't Always One "Correct" Choice

The test is designed around difficult decisions rather than obvious right and wrong answers.

Some choices may improve your chances of survival while creating difficult consequences for other people. Other choices may be compassionate but carry additional risks.

Your final results reflect the pattern of decisions you make throughout the scenario.

A Fictional Scenario

The zombie apocalypse used in this test is entirely fictional. The situations are created for entertainment and are not intended to predict how anyone would actually behave during a real disaster.

Challenge Your Friends

Once you finish the test, compare your result with your friends and see who makes it furthest.

Would your friends prioritize survival, morality, or some combination of both?

Just for Fun

The Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test is an entertainment experience. It is not a professional survival, emergency, medical, psychological, military, or personality assessment.
