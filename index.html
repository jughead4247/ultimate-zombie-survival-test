About the Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test

The Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test is a 90-decision fictional survival challenge that places you inside a developing zombie outbreak. Instead of testing your knowledge of a particular movie or television series, the test asks you to make choices as conditions change around you.

The situations cover many aspects of survival, including food, water, shelter, transportation, medical problems, dangerous encounters, resources, leadership and relationships with other survivors. Some decisions focus on immediate danger, while others require you to think about what could happen several days or weeks later.

Your decisions determine your Survival Score. Certain questions also contribute to a separate Morality Score, allowing the test to measure two different aspects of your choices rather than treating survival and morality as the same thing.

The test is designed as a fictional entertainment experience and thought experiment. Your result is not a scientific prediction of how you would perform during a real emergency or disaster.

How the Ultimate Zombie Survival Test Works

The complete test contains 90 fictional survival decisions. Every decision presents four possible choices. Read the situation carefully and select the option you believe gives you the best outcome within the scenario.

Each decision can contribute to your Survival Score. With a maximum of 5 Survival points available from each decision, the test has a maximum possible Survival Score of 450 points before the result is converted into a percentage.

Some decisions also contain a morality component. These choices contribute separately to your Morality Score and can reflect factors such as compassion, fairness, trust, responsibility and your willingness to help other people.

The two scores are intentionally separate. A player can make highly effective survival decisions while making choices that produce a lower Morality Score. Likewise, a compassionate decision is not necessarily the safest decision in every fictional situation.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the test

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first survival decision will appear with four possible choices.

Choosing an answer

Read the complete situation and all four choices before deciding. Consider both the immediate danger and the possible consequences of your choice.

Moving between decisions

Use NEXT to continue through the test. The BACK button allows you to return to an earlier decision and reconsider your previous choice before submitting the test.

Completing the test

Continue until all 90 decisions have been completed. When you are finished, select SUBMIT to calculate your Survival Score, Morality Score and final apocalypse result.

Trying again

After seeing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the test and experiment with different decisions. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to compare your results with other players.

Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test FAQ

How many decisions are in the test?

There are 90 fictional survival decisions, and every decision provides four possible choices.

What is the maximum Survival Score?

Each decision can provide up to 5 Survival points, giving the test a maximum possible Survival Score of 450 points before it is converted into a percentage.

What is the Morality Score?

The Morality Score is a separate measurement based on decisions that contain a morality component. It reflects patterns in your choices involving factors such as compassion, fairness, trust and responsibility.

Can I have a high Survival Score and a low Morality Score?

Yes. The two scores measure different aspects of your decisions. A choice that improves your chances of survival does not necessarily represent the most compassionate or fair decision.

Does every question have one correct answer?

The test is a fictional decision-making experience, not a scientific examination. Choices can involve different risks, priorities and consequences, and their point values determine how they affect your result.

Is this a real survival test?

No. Zombies and the outbreak scenario are fictional. Your result is intended for entertainment and does not predict how you would perform during a real emergency or disaster.

Is the test based on a particular zombie series?

No. The test is not based on one specific television series, movie or game. Its situations use general themes and ideas commonly found in zombie and post-apocalyptic fiction.

Can I change my answers?

Yes. You can use the BACK button to return to earlier decisions and reconsider your choices before submitting the test.

Can I take the test again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the test and see how different decisions affect your Survival and Morality Scores.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the test, use CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share the challenge and compare your apocalypse results.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz may contain spoilers for the fictional zombie-apocalypse scenarios presented throughout the test. Some decisions reveal situations, dangers, conflicts, survival challenges and possible outcomes.

If you prefer to experience each scenario without seeing its details in advance, start the test before reading further into the questions or discussing specific decisions and results with other players.
