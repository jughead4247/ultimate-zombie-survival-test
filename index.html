How Would You Survive a Zombie Apocalypse?

Think you could survive a zombie apocalypse? The Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test is a fictional zombie survival quiz where your decisions determine how well you would handle the collapse of civilization.

Face 90 difficult apocalypse decisions involving food, water, shelter, dangerous encounters, other survivors, resource management, leadership, and long-term survival.

Your decisions determine your Survival Score. Some decisions also reveal something about your moral choices and contribute to a separate Morality Score.

What You'll Face in the Zombie Survival Quiz

The 90-decision survival test puts you in situations where every choice can affect your chances of surviving.

  • Finding and managing food and water
  • Choosing safe shelters and routes
  • Dealing with zombies and dangerous situations
  • Deciding whether to trust other survivors
  • Managing limited resources
  • Making difficult moral decisions
  • Building a long-term survival strategy

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the zombie survival quiz work?

You face a series of fictional zombie-apocalypse situations. Each situation gives you four choices. Your decisions affect your survival result.

How many questions are in the zombie apocalypse survival test?

The complete test contains 90 decisions. Each decision presents four choices that can affect your Survival Score, and selected decisions also contribute to your Morality Score.

What is the Survival Score?

Your Survival Score represents how well your choices perform across the survival scenarios. The final version uses a maximum of 450 Survival points.

What is the Morality Score?

Morality is measured only on decisions where your choice actually reveals something about your values. Not every survival decision contributes to morality.

Is this a real survival test?

No. This is fictional entertainment and does not predict how someone would perform during a real disaster or emergency.

Can I take the test again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result and make different decisions.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the test and see who makes the best survival decisions.
